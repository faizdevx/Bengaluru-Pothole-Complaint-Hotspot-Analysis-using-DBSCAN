What this is

An analysis of pothole-related civic complaints in Bengaluru: where reports concentrate and which areas repeatedly show elevated complaint activity. It analyses reported complaints. It does not detect potholes, measure depth or pavement quality, or claim that complaint density equals road-condition severity.

DBSCAN

DBSCAN groups points that have at least min_samples neighbours within eps metres (haversine distance). It needs no preset cluster count, finds irregular shapes, and labels isolated reports as noise (label −1), which is kept and reported. Its weakness here: one global density threshold under-detects sparse areas and can merge dense ones. See DBSCAN Parameters.

Dataset provenance

  • Source: Janaagraha / I Change My City, “Complaints Log 2019–2022” (CC BY-SA). Host: OpenCity. It is a civic-tech portal log, not a BBMP real-time API, and not a live feed.
  • Boundary: union of BBMP 2015 ward polygons (198 wards), via OpenCity.
  • Secondary: BBMP Fix My Street (May–June 2022), used only for validation and kept separate.
  • The file ends 2022-07-31. Free-text and address fields are dropped; no complainant data is stored or shown.

Reporting bias

Complaints reflect who uses the portal and when: adoption, awareness, neighbourhood engagement and population all differ across Bengaluru, and portal use collapsed after 2019. Normalising by all complaints in the same unit and month helps but does not remove these effects.

Limitations

  • Complaint concentration is not pothole density or severity; there is no physical ground truth.
  • Historical data only; sparse (most reports are noise); small clusters are weak evidence.
  • Some coordinates are locality-level or default pins; a rule-based exclusion is used and is a heuristic.
  • The city boundary is the 2015 ward union; reports outside it are excluded.
  • DBSCAN uses a global density threshold; parameter choice is a judgement (see the sweep).

Terminology

The interface reports complaint activity, spatial density, persistence and recent activity. It never reports pothole “severity”.
